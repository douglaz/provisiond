## Fable's dissent, traced in full

Fable ACCEPTS C-UNKNOWN and NEITHER, rejects C-WITNESS, and explicitly prefers NEITHER without any repaired form. Its main objection is paying availability/evidence costs before reconsidering the breadth and trust of the existing historical classification. The paired trace is valid: actually clean and event-hidden histories present the same reports; a deterministic survivor-only restart is actually conforming in the former and violates the historical arm in the latter. Retention does not add a knowledge predicate. Abstention prevents the immediate bad promotion but does not complete recovery or satisfy the required successful restart control. C-UNKNOWN intentionally changes the clean-but-unproved verdict, including an operator's attempt to call it a restart; a person may instead authorize the existing restore.

The breadth is existing policy, not a premise this panel accidentally introduced: audit “no stated expiry or catch-up reset”. The stricter historical negative-evidence standard is an openly substantive new requirement, not a runtime replication-check amendment. Fable's suggested alternative of trusting the individual-termination prohibition and recording only shutdown paths is unprinted, has no counted vote, and is not recommended here.

Fable says the candidates contradict the earlier owner option “Promote either as a restart, so tokens stay valid”. The complete option at `r74/decisions/pv-1i3n.md:18` continues “all existing guards and known-violation handling stay”. T2 says “This changes no evidence requirement”; T5 says “They introduce no new historical-evidence mechanism”. Thus the earlier choice did not settle this unknown-history policy. The amendments *would change* that policy and costs, which is why owner approval is required; no clean/equal *established* path is removed.

Fable also identifies outside edits. Checked separately:

- **OVR-19:** its actual universal is “Every value the set says a deployment MUST state is listed here”. C-WITNESS does say “The deployment MUST name the trusted issuer, the validation procedure and the failure domains”; the unamended register has no such row. This is a genuine unresolved integration obligation for the unselected WITNESS package. C-UNKNOWN contains no deployment MUST-name/state instruction for an issuer or new configurable value. Extending WITNESS's row objection to C-UNKNOWN is unsupported; the existing endpoint-procedure rows concern reporting, not an implicit new parameter. No register row is silently added to either form.
- **OVR-18:** the quoted current text enumerates particular alarms and says “Admission exhaustion and accounting loss MUST be alarmed”. It contains no universal requiring every new operator report to acquire an OVR-18 alarm hook. C-UNKNOWN's “MUST report the unresolved condition to the operator” is itself the new owner duty and its check asserts operator notice. An extra alarm or threshold would be a distinct policy, not a required hidden repair.
- **CONTEXT:** no “incarnation” glossary entry was found, as the readers report. No cited convention makes absence of every newly used word a contradiction. The affected database/source history and continuity restrictions are expressed in the complete candidate. A narrower definition or different inheritance horizon would need its own exact reviewed form, not an opportunistic implementation edit.
- **ADR/history:** decision/amendment records follow an adopted rule, as expressly disclosed before launch. Nothing has been adopted this turn.
