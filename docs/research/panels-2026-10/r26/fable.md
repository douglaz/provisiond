**Verdict: (c).** Count only authoritative observations. Retire the universal in Decision 1, record the residual, add no state.

**Why.** The gap is real but it is not the tenant's to open. Every refresh failure the brief lists is a provider-side or operator-side condition. A 5xx and a timeout are the provider's. A 401 is the operator's credential. A 429 cannot come from the deployment's own traffic once Decision 3 spends the declared limit locally "before any provider call" (ADR-0034:39-40), only from something else holding the project token. A 404 past the create's window is an observation, not a failure: `03-operation-lifecycle.md:1066` lists "a refresh" among the gone-write triggers, and the gone-write stamps `state_observed_at`. Every verb a tenant could use to provoke a throttle is ceilinged (`Admission.lean:222-235`, plus Decision 2). So the loop runs only while the provider is already failing that machine's reads, and `OPS-8` serializes it to about one failed read a second against a bucket that refills at one a second (ADR-0034:8). What it drains is the tenant share, never the reserve; ADR-0034:65 already says that share's exhaustion is "accepted, priced to the attacker, and alarmed". The money harm the ADR exists for, stalled deletes, is closed by Decision 3 alone. Decision 1's bound is load-bearing only for pricing between tenants, and under (c) the price falls from "many paid machines" to "one paid machine, during a provider read failure you did not cause". That residual is not worth a column, a pacing contract, or a `succeeded` refresh that read nothing.

**Defects in the framing.**

1. ADR-0034:66-67 says "The caller already reads its age from `state_observed_at`". False on the wire. `WIR-11`'s machine view (`13-wire-contract.md:295-313`) carries `updated_at` and `network_restriction.observed_at` and no `state_observed_at`. `STO-48` (`05-persistence.md:279`) says `updated_at` "cannot stand in for it". Option (a)'s "returning the cached state with its true `state_observed_at`" assumes a field that does not exist. Any option, (c) included, needs this field for the settled Decision 1 to be honest.
2. Confirming the retraction: `write_started_at` is "Null on every kind `OPS-45` does not reach" (`05-persistence.md:372`) and `OPS-45` scopes itself to six mutating kinds (`03-operation-lifecycle.md:1208-1211`). The record that does exist is the failed refresh's own `operations` row, retained under `STO-14` (`05-persistence.md:1359`), so (a) could be a query rather than a column. But a budget-refused refresh and a restart-settled one are also `failed` rows (`03-operation-lifecycle.md:235-238`; `Restore.lean:338`), so "failed refresh within window" means attempted or refused, not dispatched.
3. ADR-0035 does not touch refresh. `03-operation-lifecycle.md:260-262`: "The refresh, parent, install/rescue-inventory and admission-only rows are unchanged". `Tables.lean:225` proves `refresh_always_failed` by `rfl` over every failure and marker.
4. "404 inside `PRV-36`'s window" misreads `PRV-36`. It governs "a mutation the driver issued" (`02-provider-contract.md:824-825`), and a refresh issues none. The window that matters is the create's, as `OPS-32` reads it (`03-operation-lifecycle.md:1340-1343`), seconds long, on a machine seconds old.
5. (b) collides with `API-50`. A caller "that obeys every `Retry-After` it receives MUST never receive `429` for throughput" (`04-api-contract.md:1185`, `CNF-152`). A `failed` refresh is terminal, so it carries no `poll_after_ms` (`13-wire-contract.md:235-236`), and `retry_after_ms` is keyed to four kinds (`13-wire-contract.md:131-134`), none of which a provider 5xx maps to. The second refresh would be throttled for rate with no prior pacing. The Manager's "error/pacing contract" is this carve-out, and it is bigger than the sentence suggests.
6. (a) misleads the caller the brief worries about. A refresh issued because the last one failed settles `succeeded` with `{}` (`13-wire-contract.md:277`) having read nothing, and only defect 1's missing field could tell the caller so.
7. The one zero-state shape that keeps the universal, holding the failed refresh `running` as a cooldown, is the lock `ADR-0016` removed, and it would defer exposure-reducing deletes behind a refresh (`03-operation-lifecycle.md:188-190`). Not viable.
