**Verdict: A — the operator absorbs provider rounding.**

The ten-minute example exposes a real loss, but B turns a provider invoice rule into customer “machine time” after the machine has stopped billing. The accepted price rule applies a percentage to machine time, and the meter prices elapsed billable seconds; neither promises to pass through every rounded provider invoice amount ([ADR-0007:5](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/docs/adr/0007-margin-is-a-percentage-of-machine-time-privileged-operations-are-free.md:5), [LDG-38:628](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:628)). Keep that promise and treat rounding as an explicit operator pricing risk. A disclosed minimum charge per machine could be a later pricing decision; it would be more honest than calling time after deletion usage.

- **The story is true for a short Hetzner machine, but the stop is not necessarily 12:10.** Dispatch does not end customer billing. An ordinary exit closes the increment in the write recording its stop; a gone observation normally stops it at the *observation instant*. A scheduled cancellation stops at the earlier effective date or gone observation. `stopped` and `cancellation_scheduled` remain billable ([LDG-38:873](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:873), [LDG-74:510](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:510), [LDG-37:471](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:471)). Provider billing stop is a separately evidenced fact ([PRV-29:263](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/02-provider-contract.md:263)).

- **“Provider wins” does not authorize B.** `LDG-37` calls the provider’s usage API a possible source for *elapsed billable time*; `LDG-28` says “Debits round up,” meaning ledger units, not provider hours ([LDG-37:455](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:455), [LDG-28:46](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:46)). `wind_down_cost` prices cancellation delay and margin, not an already-started hour ([PRV-13b:226](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/02-provider-contract.md:226)). `LDG-66` records operator deficiencies without changing tenant balances; `LDG-75` can expose an invoice difference, but neither reimburses the operator ([LDG-66:1373](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:1373), [LDG-75:1940](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:1940)).

- **B assumes a unit grid the evidence does not establish.** Hetzner Cloud describes rounded *server usage* subject to a monthly cap; it does not specify a charge at every exit from a local billable state. Robot says hour calculation starts when access is given, which argues against a wall-clock-hour anchor, but its monthly invoice boundary still needs checking. Neither supports rounding every stop/re-entry span ([Cloud FAQ](https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/billing/faq/), [Robot billing FAQ](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/billing-and-account-management/billing-at-hetzner/billing-system-hetzner/)). A one-unit minimum per span, or charging its first unit at entry, has the same unproved per-span premise. A unit at every exit could bill rounding Hetzner never applied, especially across the UTC month split or after the monthly cap ([LDG-68:57](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:57)). C changes the margin, not this defect. Robot’s “add-ons” example also does not establish the billing rule for each attachment ([research §3:590](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md:590)).

- **B breaks established exceptional exits.** A provider-terminated machine stops at evidenced absence, not a future unit end; attachments continue as separate subjects ([LDG-74:479](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:479), [LDG-74:526](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:526)). A quarantined exit posts no usage debit, and a rate-outage window is operator-borne and never billed later ([LDG-72:1013](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:1013), [LDG-64:1302](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/12-billing-and-ledger.md:1302)). One reserved unit would not cover arbitrarily repeated exits if each created a charge; the current commitment is otherwise fixed ([ADR-0011:24](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/docs/adr/0011-the-commitment-is-fixed-runway-floats.md:24)).

- **A has a material, measurable cost.** With a 20% margin, hourly provider price \(H\), lifetime \(t\), and no cap or delay, loss occurs when \(1.2t < \lceil t/\text{hour}\rceil\text{ hour}\). In the first hour that is \(t<50\) minutes; ten minutes loses \(0.8H\), and the per-machine loss approaches \(H\) as \(t\) approaches zero. Robot’s stated limit is 20 orders/day **on each** of two endpoints, shared by the deployment: at most 40 such hourly losses/day if both channels are usable by one tenant. The set gives no comparable numeric Cloud create ceiling or fixed per-tenant maximum; its general tenant API limit is only a SHOULD ([PRV-40:874](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/02-provider-contract.md:874), [API-29:1154](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/04-api-contract.md:1154)).

- **The measurement claim needs separating.** An invoice may reveal a rounded amount, but that residual is not a request-to-*actual billing-stop latency* sample; rounding can mask latency rather than measure it. Conversely, saying twenty samples “cannot reveal” rounding is too categorical. `F18`’s seconds procedure needs independent stop evidence, with invoice checks for unit, anchor and cap ([PRV-13b:162](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/02-provider-contract.md:162), [F18:1603](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/11-open-findings.md:1603), [research §3:546](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md:546)). Under A, **do not add a unit to the tenant reserve**: released commitment cannot pay an operator-borne cost. `PRV-13c` already names billing granularity as a per-contract fact, but `PRV-44` has no unit field; add one only if the engine will use it ([PRV-13c:395](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/02-provider-contract.md:395), [PRV-44:59](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r18/sol/02-provider-contract.md:59)).
