**A — absorb tariff rounding explicitly; keep elapsed-time billing.**

The cost mismatch is real, but invoice rounding is not necessarily time after deletion. B turns an incompletely established tariff into fictional machine lifetime. - **The grid is unproven.** Hetzner documents elapsed usage beginning at availability, hourly rounding and monthly caps—not wall-clock-hour occupancy ([billing rules](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/billing-and-account-management/billing-at-hetzner/billing-system-hetzner/), [Cloud FAQ](https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/billing/faq/)). Per-resource, per-month ceiling is a reasonable inference; exact boundary carry/reset needs consumption-statement evidence. The supplied quotes do not settle it ([research:565](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md#L565)). If usage aggregates, separately rounding 50+10 minutes charges two hours where one suffices. At the cap, additional runtime can cost nothing. “One unit per machine” can fail if month fragments round separately. LDG-68’s “calendar month in UTC” establishes provisiond’s boundary, not provider buckets ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:57](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L57)).

- **Exits are not every state change.** “`stopped` is billable”; so is `cancellation_scheduled` ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:471](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L471)). Separate tariffs and cleanup defeat a single machine-unit allowance. C fixes none of these problems.

- **DigitalOcean is not merely a second-sized unit.** Its minimum is the greater of 60 seconds’ cost and $0.01 ([pricing](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/droplets/details/pricing/)). At an illustrative $0.01/hour, the monetary floor binds for an hour. The research conclusion “less than a minute” is false ([research:552](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md#L552)).

- **A’s economics:** assuming stable rates, one uncapped aggregation period, hourly provider price \(p\), lifetime \(t\) hours and markup \(m\), profit is \(p[(1+m)t-\lceil t\rceil]\), ignoring monetary rounding/overheads. At 20%, loss intervals in minutes are `(0,50), (60,100), (120,150), (180,200), (240,250)`. Ten minutes loses **0.8p**, not fifty minutes’ net cost. ADR-0007’s “one box for two hours” earns **0.4p** ([ADR-0007:30](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/docs/adr/0007-margin-is-a-percentage-of-machine-time.md#L30)). No finite markup covers arbitrarily short rentals.

- **Loops have a rate bound, not a lifetime bound.** SEC-39 requires “ceilings per principal,” “default one hour,” without a universal integer ([07-security-requirements.md:209](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/07-security-requirements.md#L209)); a customer principal is “exactly one tenant” ([04-api-contract.md:145](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/04-api-contract.md#L145)). With \(C\) short creates per interval and unit cost \(U\), loss approaches \(CU\). Illustratively, ten/hour and €0.06/unit approach €0.60/hour. PRV-40’s “20 requests per day” is shared per Robot ordering endpoint, not a Cloud tenant limit ([02-provider-contract.md:874](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/02-provider-contract.md#L874)).

- **Smaller alternatives:** a disclosed one-unit minimum fixes sub-unit rentals, not later rounding jumps. Charging it at entry needs crediting against subsequent usage. A provider-unit minimum differs from the **meter-interval** minimum rejected by ADR-0011 ([ADR-0011:88](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/docs/adr/0011-fixed-commitment-floating-runway.md#L88)). Provider usage helps only with established aggregation and timely evidence.

- **Measurement and reserves:** neither “cannot reveal” nor “would see it” follows universally ([research:546](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md#L546)). Invoice quantities reveal extra cost without necessarily revealing a stop timestamp. Separate latency from contractual rounding/minimum/cap evidence; twenty samples cannot establish the contractual maximum. Current sizing already uses `max(declared, max(observed))` ([02-provider-contract.md:170](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/02-provider-contract.md#L170)). Both A and B need exposure budgeting; under A, **operator capital**, not refundable customer commitments, funds rounding. B needs authorized residual liability plus latency and attachments, without double-counting.
