**Verdict: other — driver-reported outcome uncertainty; reject (a) as written.**

At `3e4b6fa`, the classification defect is real. The missing distinction is between rejection of the requested mutation and failure to observe its outcome. Have the driver report that distinction while preserving the underlying error kind, status and identifiers. A known rejection stays `failed`; an inconclusive observation after acceptance enters reconciliation. This closes the hole across operation kinds without treating every dispatched request as an unknown outcome.
