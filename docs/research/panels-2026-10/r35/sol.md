**Reject** — the proposed scope leaves the IPv4 sale decision unresolved, while the attachment customer rate that the new bound is meant to fund does not exist in v1.

- **Robot order trace.** Without `primary_ipv4`, the order buys no IPv4 add-on; the driver needs the IPv6 rescue path allowed by [RSC-45](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/06-rescue-install.md#L501). Robot’s declared Additional IP and subnet kinds are possible survivors, not instances on every order: [PRV-13a](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/02-provider-contract.md#L190) says, “The declaration names kinds, not an assertion that every instance survives.” That declaration alone should not reach `offer_not_sellable`. With `primary_ipv4`, the order buys a separately billed *Primary IPv4* product whose cost [RSC-45](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/06-rescue-install.md#L513) puts in the machine offer price. Hetzner distinguishes Primary IPv4 from Additional IPs in its [official FAQ](https://docs.hetzner.com/robot/dedicated-server/ip/faq-primary-ipv4/), but [PRV-13a’s Robot row](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/02-provider-contract.md#L195) names only Additional IP and subnet. Whether the add-on survives server deletion remains the unrun [batch B step 9](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md#L688). If it survives and keeps costing money, it is `billable` under [the attachment schema](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/05-persistence.md#L340); its cleanup bound or cap is then unresolved. The fix cannot yet establish that this order avoids `offer_not_sellable`.

- **The proposed scope is not an admission input.** [PRV-44](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/02-provider-contract.md#L74) declares bounds by surviving kind, but no per-offer set or count of attachments an order creates. [PRV-45](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/02-provider-contract.md#L527) discovers actual survivors at deletion. V1 has no tenant add route—[WIR-52](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/13-wire-contract.md#L862) exposes read and release—but a later route or an out-of-band provider addition would escape an order-only rule. It needs admission when added, or treatment as operator exposure. A pointer in `PRV-45` also cannot narrow [LDG-32’s](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L329) rule about an offer’s attachments.

- **The two bounds answer different questions.** [LDG-32](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L329) requires “bounded automatic cleanup”; an effective-date bound does not prove eventual absence or release of the commitment. Nor does `now` alone justify zero: Robot’s [IP cancellation API](https://robot.hetzner.com/doc/webservice/en.html) permits `now` and returns a calendar date, which Q18a interprets as that day’s end if carried as an effective date. [PRV-45](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/02-provider-contract.md#L538) distinguishes a completed release without a date from one carrying a date.

- **There is no calculable attachment reserve or specified carve-out.** [PRV-13b](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/02-provider-contract.md#L244) and [LDG-33](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L345) price attachment time, yet [the attachment row](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/05-persistence.md#L334) has no rate, [DOM-9](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/01-domain-model.md#L189) gives an offer one recurring price, and [LDG-24](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L1832) applies margin to machine time only. Count × rate × bound cannot be computed. [LDG-32’s](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L329) “explicitly accepted and capped” exception has no acceptance record or cap on [OVR-19’s register](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/00-overview.md#L350); [LDG-31’s clamp](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L280) limits customer debit after a shortfall, not provider liability. Q18a also needs a pre-order gate before the future cancellation branch still described in [PRV-13c](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/8c3087e8c6085b922eda16be2c3bcd3a0d87372c/02-provider-contract.md#L498) is enabled.
