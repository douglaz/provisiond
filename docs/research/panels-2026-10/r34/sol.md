**Q18a verdict — choose (b) for v1, with a required operator correction when the date is behind the mark.** Leaving the posted overcharge in place, (c), conflicts with ADR-0037’s “The customer owes the attachment’s rate through the effective date” ([ADR-0037:124](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/docs/adr/0037-the-billing-stop-window-is-withdrawn-and-wind-down-is-sized-to-the-gone-write.md#L124)). The mark discard prevents another debit; it does not undo one.

The case is **not proved unreachable**. Robot is the only currently saleable driver ([ADR-0039:61](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/docs/adr/0039-only-offers-with-no-traffic-overage-are-sold-until-a-counter-is-proven-fresh.md#L61)). Its [IP and subnet cancellation API](https://robot.hetzner.com/doc/webservice/en.html#post-ip-ip-cancellation) accepts “now” or a date and reports the cancellation date. The spec declares additional IPs and subnets *possible* Robot survivors, and tests a survivor as well as a machine with none ([02-provider-contract.md:190](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/02-provider-contract.md#L190), [10-conformance-checklist.md:3381](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/10-conformance-checklist.md#L3381)). I found no documented scheduled attachment release for the other drivers. The brief’s claim that v1 orders no extra IPs does not establish that none can be attached or discovered later; the cited research says the driver orders **no licences**, while leaving Robot IP survival for a live test ([provider-verify-2026-10.md:554](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md#L554)).

The strongest reason against (a) is that it is **new calculation and workflow machinery**, although `correction` is an existing entry kind. A usage entry stores an increment *end*, amount and period, but no start; an increment may leave no ledger entry at all ([05-persistence.md:560](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/05-persistence.md#L560), [05-persistence.md:1090](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/05-persistence.md#L1090)). Thus “credit 10 sats” assumes ten sats were actually posted: the clamp may have reduced the debit ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:281](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L281)). Partial entries, rate splits and rounding need evidence the current entry does not retain. `LDG-62` is “Extending runway,” **not** a correction path ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:1901](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L1901)).

For (b), make the dated terminal or `applied` resolution flag the case for operator review; the operator posts a positive `correction` naming each affected debit in a separate transaction under `LDG-35`’s tenant serialization. `LDG-8` requires a tenant-unique key but supplies no key for this correction, so specify one derived from the attachment, corrected entry and effective date, with a rule for revised dates ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:131](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L131)). Attribute each correction to its original entry’s UTC period, even if posted after month end ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:879](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L879)). It raises available balance whether the commitment is open or closed; it neither reopens the commitment nor rewinds the mark. `LDG-38` already says “A `correction` leaves the meter’s state untouched” ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:833](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L833)). If (a) is chosen instead, its writer must be the terminal/resolution path, with the date and credits atomic under that same serialization; it also needs durable interval evidence and a date-revision rule.

**Q18b verdict — refuse sale without a declared bound.** ADR-0037’s “or the shortfall meets `LDG-31`’s clamp” describes a consequence; it does not explicitly grant admission ([ADR-0037:125](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/docs/adr/0037-the-billing-stop-window-is-withdrawn-and-wind-down-is-sized-to-the-gone-write.md#L125)). The governing first principle is `PRV-13b`: a prepaid reseller “MUST be able to bound its own exposure before taking money” ([02-provider-contract.md:202](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/02-provider-contract.md#L202)). `PRV-45` already refuses an unbounded kind ([02-provider-contract.md:551](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/02-provider-contract.md#L551)); the clamp protects the customer if an *actual* date exceeds a declared bound. The binary question misses another input: a time bound **per kind** does not size the reserve without a bound on attachment count and rates when rows are only discovered at deletion ([02-provider-contract.md:238](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/02-provider-contract.md#L238), [02-provider-contract.md:527](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/33bb62faef761cd9c1c58c4abe30b7f9fdacd320/02-provider-contract.md#L527)).

**Scope verdict — neither question dissolves on the present text.** Deferral needs a proved, enforced no-scheduled-survivor condition for every saleable offer, reconciled with the Robot survivor declaration and test above. An absent extra-IP ordering verb alone is insufficient.
