**Verdict: (d) — keep the existing order budget, alarm on depletion, and measure demand before adding admission rules.**

The scarce thing is an order *request*, while the proposed price controls charge for an *accepted machine*. Until Hetzner confirms whether rejected and `test=true` requests consume quota, neither extra prepaid time nor a tenant share is a reliable defence. A daily tenant share adds friction, but anonymous tenants can acquire more shares with credit they can later spend. Pacing is the smallest plausible third shape: it limits a burst’s immediate effect, but an attacker can take each newly available slot, and an honest launch burst is delayed too. Committed runway is released on deletion, so it does not raise attack cost. Charging extra, nonrefundable prepaid units would raise cost only by pricing time the provider did not charge; that changes the accepted first-unit rule and [ADR-0007’s single machine-time multiplier](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/docs/adr/0007-margin-is-a-percentage-of-machine-time.md:5). Holding slots for established tenants favours account history without proving distinct people under [ADR-0005](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/docs/adr/0005-collect-nothing.md:5).

**Defects and limits in the framing**

- [Robot’s reference](https://robot.hetzner.com/doc/webservice/en.html) lists **20 requests per day** at each of the standard and auction order routes. It does **not** establish independent counters, account versus webservice-user scope, rolling versus calendar reset, or whether rejected and `test=true` requests count. Its `test` description says the order is not processed, which does not answer the quota question. A local refusal makes no Robot request; a provider rejection might consume a slot for no machine charge. [PRV-40](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/02-provider-contract.md:874) asserts simulated conformance orders spend the budget without cited counter evidence. For the same reason, more accounts add capacity only if Hetzner confirms separate counters.

- The **€2/day** figure is a scenario, not a verified attack price. [Two September auction orders cost about €0.16 total](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/08-provider-notes.md:378). Twenty at €0.08 provider cost would cost the customer `€1.60 × (1 + margin)` for the first hours, about €1.92 at an illustrative 20% margin, plus applicable conversion and add-ons. [ADR-0033](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/docs/adr/0033-a-machines-first-billing-unit-is-prepaid-machine-time.md:26) debits that first unit on accepted orders. [PRV-13b](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/02-provider-contract.md:175) also commits runway and wind-down; commitment is not itself a charge, and unused balance is released on deletion. Auction offers are individual machines that [leave the feed when purchased](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/08-provider-notes.md:390), so twenty orders at the observed cheapest price are not assured. Immediate cancellation does not undo the first-hour debit.

- The standard channel is **not categorically self-limiting through setup fees**. The [September live-catalogue snapshot](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/08-provider-notes.md:140) found €59–€1349 net across its orderable products, making €1,180 in fees for twenty at that snapshot’s minimum. Hetzner’s [published limited-offer price list](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/) also contains standard models with **€0 setup fee**. It does not prove those limited models are orderable now; the live offer must decide. A rejected request that counts would bypass either fee altogether.

- [API-35](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/04-api-contract.md:457) requires a configured activation minimum “large enough to purchase something” but gives no amount; [OVR-19’s register](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/00-overview.md:350) omits it. If a proposed share is one order per tenant, twenty orders need twenty funded tenants, but their deposits remain spendable on those orders or later compute under [ADR-0004](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/docs/adr/0004-the-regulatory-perimeter-is-a-product-constraint.md:18). Unspent credit ties up money; it is not twenty additional fees.

- [OPS-20 was withdrawn](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/docs/adr/0017-the-episode-is-an-entity-and-requeue-is-deleted.md:52). [OPS-27](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/sol/03-operation-lifecycle.md:426) searches transactions and reads a matched machine; resolution places no new order. Honest creates and conformance orders can exhaust the same capacity without an attacker. The reference also lists a **third** 20/day order route for add-ons, relevant if that channel is later used. The strongest case against more machinery is therefore concrete: one route has at most twenty documented request slots per day, while there is no measured Robot demand or verified cheap-request counter behaviour against which to size a fair share.
