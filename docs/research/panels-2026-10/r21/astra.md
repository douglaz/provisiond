**(d) — accept and alarm on create unavailability; clarify quota accounting before adding allocation or pricing policy.**

The first-principles question is what distinguishes an attacker’s funded purchase from an honest purchase. Tenant shares do not establish that distinction, and refundable commitments do not buy lasting deterrence. Preserve the settled billing decision, measure quota consumption and refused demand, and revisit when the lost sales justify another commercial restriction.

- **The quota’s scope and reset are unverified.** The [Robot reference](https://robot.hetzner.com/doc/webservice/en.html) documents “20 requests per day” separately on both server-order POST endpoints. It does **not** establish account versus credential/IP scope, independent buckets, rolling versus calendar reset, rejected-request accounting, or test-request accounting. Consequently, neither “until the window rolls” nor “each account adds 20” is established. `PRV-40`’s “whole deployment” disagrees with the register’s “per ordering account” ([02-provider-contract.md:879](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/02-provider-contract.md:879), [00-overview.md:380](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/00-overview.md:380)). `SEC-43` already requires “multiple provider accounts”; extra credentials alone prove no extra quota ([07-security-requirements.md:323](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/07-security-requirements.md:323)).

- **Standard orders are not intrinsically expensive.** Hetzner publishes **AX41-1-LTD and EX44-1-LTD with zero setup fees**, availability permitting. Their presence in today’s authenticated Robot catalogue remains unverified. Your dated observation is not a durable €1,200 attack floor. [Provider price table](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/).

- **€2 is a conditional accepted-order estimate, not the cheapest demonstrated attack.** For confirmed auction purchases cancelled within their prepaid hour, expenditure is approximately \(\sum_i (1+m)h_i\), converted to satoshis. The recorded €0.08 examples imply **€1.92 for twenty at an illustrative 20% margin**; they do not establish today’s twenty cheapest available offers ([08-provider-notes.md:378](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/08-provider-notes.md:378)). `ADR-0033` says “debited on confirmed acceptance”; cancellation does not refund that debit ([ADR-0033:28](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/docs/adr/0033-a-machines-first-billing-unit-is-prepaid-machine-time.md:28)). Admission additionally needs runway, wind-down and applicable attachment reserves ([02-provider-contract.md:175](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/02-provider-contract.md:175): “requested_runway × customer_rate”). These increase required funding, not necessarily expenditure: `LDG-32` releases the “remaining amount” when billing, including attachments, stops ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:297](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:297)). These ADR amendments remain listed as “Edits owed.”

- **Rejected orders may defeat every accepted-order price.** If rejected POSTs consume quota, an admitted stale/sold-out offer could consume a slot for **zero debit**: `LDG-39` says “Deterministic rejection before acceptance” is “Never debited” ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:1081](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:1081)). Reachability and repeatability need verification. A purely local refusal makes no upstream request; charging it against shared capacity would create a separate local denial path. Test requests buy nothing, but tenant access to testing is not established: `PRV-34` requires a live purchase “sets `test=false` exactly once” ([02-provider-contract.md:731](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/02-provider-contract.md:731)). Immediate cancellation after delivery still incurs an hour; pre-delivery transaction cancellation is not documented. [Billing rules](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/billing-and-account-management/billing-at-hetzner/billing-system-hetzner/).

- **(a) limits accidents better than Sybils.** With daily share \(q\), exhausting twenty slots needs \(\lceil20/q\rceil\) identities. If activation minimum is \(A\), their entry funding floor is \(A\lceil20/q\rceil\), plus sufficient purchase capacity. But `API-35` tests “cumulative credited balance” ([04-api-contract.md:457](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/04-api-contract.md:457)); `ADR-0004` makes it “spendable only on the operator’s own compute” ([ADR-0004:23](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/docs/adr/0004-the-regulatory-perimeter-is-a-product-constraint.md:23)). That credit pays the attack’s orders. Do not add it again as a fee. Unused abandoned credit costs money; identities reused over many days amortize that cost.

- **The third shapes have different effects.** Pacing is the smallest additional control: twenty/day averages **one slot per 72 minutes**, preventing immediate exhaustion but letting an attacker contest every release. Committed-runway admission raises recyclable capital requirements. Neither raises irreversible expenditure. Non-refundable **24-hour prepayment** would raise the illustrative accepted-order attack to **€46.08**, but also charge honest short-lived machines more. It changes `ADR-0033`’s “provider’s first billing unit,” and reopens `ADR-0011`’s “overcharges partial use” objection ([ADR-0033:63](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/docs/adr/0033-a-machines-first-billing-unit-is-prepaid-machine-time.md:63)). It can retain one multiplier; a separate attempt fee conflicts with “no per-operation accounting” ([ADR-0007:19](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/docs/adr/0007-margin-is-a-percentage-of-machine-time.md:19)). Ledger-based history need not identify customers; identity linkage conflicts with “collects no customer identity” ([ADR-0005:6](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/docs/adr/0005-collect-nothing.md:6)). Age can be farmed; paid history buys sunk expenditure.

- **Recovery does not purchase again.** `OPS-20` is withdrawn: “deleted the verb” ([README.md:241](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/README.md:241)). `OPS-27` “searches the provider” ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:427](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/03-operation-lifecycle.md:427)); those reads consume their own request allowances. Conformance orders, including simulations, consume the local budget under `PRV-40`’s “including a simulated one” ([02-provider-contract.md:881](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r21/astra/02-provider-contract.md:881)). An honest launch burst of **21 requests** already exceeds a twenty-slot allowance.

**Strongest don’t-build case:** twenty daily purchases permit **600 purchases per thirty days**; at thirty-day average lifetimes, roughly **600 concurrent machines per independent quota scope**. Demand and churn determine whether this matters. No allocation rule creates capacity.
