**Verdict: Make future-constrained offers explicitly unsellable in v1; if the owner intends to sell them, fund the priced-through day before ordering.**

The midnight gap is real in the written rules. At 23:30, the Oct 31 start is only 30 minutes away, so the start-based exception test treats it as ordinary. Yet the runway formula protects cost through Oct 31’s end. With a commitment too small to cover that protected amount, usable runway becomes zero. The Manager’s end-based test would repair funding **if that offer is sold**.

The first-principles question changes the recommendation. A *permitted-from* day is not itself a scheduled stop. Day-end cost follows when a cancellation **dated for that day** remains effective until day-end; an immediate cancellation after midnight can instead stop at an earlier gone observation. Hetzner’s [dedicated-server guide](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/billing-and-account-management/cancellation/cancellations-robot/) says “You can only cancel immediately,” while its [Robot API](https://robot.hetzner.com/doc/webservice/en.html) exposes both a dated cancellation parameter and an earliest date. Neither establishes what `now` does before a future earliest date. Research V12 says current products give no such test machine, not that future dates are impossible ([provider-verify-2026-10.md:328](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/12a1c898b88835a4cfca9eb9979dbde65614c93c/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md#L328)).
