**Q13 — BLOCKER: use maximum for overlapping terms, but reject `max(deadline, window)` as an unconditional bound on wind-down.**

The specified window does **not** start after the reply: PRV-13b says “dispatch to the recorded gone” ([02-provider-contract.md:195](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/02-provider-contract.md#L195)); STO-53 records “when the mutation was dispatched” and “when the engine first observed its effect” ([05-persistence.md:479](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/05-persistence.md#L479)). Consequently, `D + V` double-counts overlapping time when both describe that dispatch.

However, reply waiting and subsequent evidence calls can be sequential:

- **Hetzner Cloud:** DELETE returns an Action; its schema admits running, success and error. Acceptance therefore need not supply gone evidence. ([Official schema](https://docs.hetzner.cloud/cloud.spec.json))
- **Robot:** `cancellation_date=now` requests immediate cancellation; inspect the actual result, because the repository explicitly retains rejection/automatic-scheduling uncertainty for constrained contracts ([08-provider-notes.md:151](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/08-provider-notes.md#L151)). The [Robot API](https://robot.hetzner.com/doc/webservice/en.html#post-server-server-number-cancellation) exposes cancellation status/date.
- Lost or insufficient replies require PRV-29’s “authoritative, non-mutating provider evidence,” interpreted through PRV-36 ([02-provider-contract.md:281](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/02-provider-contract.md#L281)). **OPS-27’s correlator search is a create path**, not a delete correlator: “Resolution searches … the operation’s correlator” ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:446](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L446)). LDG-74 supplies the eventual meter boundary: “Billing stops at the observation instant” ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:545](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L545)).

A reply at 600 followed by gone evidence at 630 makes **30 an invalid dispatch-to-gone bound**; it does not move V’s origin. Widening future samples cannot repair that reserve retrospectively. Worse, a confirming call dispatched at 600 has its own deadline: one such sequential read can require allowance through 1200, before local overhead. OVR-19 explicitly says expiry “establishes neither the mutation’s outcome nor that billing stopped” ([00-overview.md:417](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/00-overview.md#L417)).

**Chosen repair:** PRV-13b owns maximum for shared-origin waits plus explicitly identified sequential work **not already included in V**, retaining detection, machine-wait and margin. Amend OVR-19’s pointer and CNF-329 with delayed-reply/confirming-read traces. “Including when either is larger” proves neither composition ([10-conformance-checklist.md:3139](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/10-conformance-checklist.md#L3139)).

**Third shape / don’t build:** require a conservative end-to-end V covering the complete successful path. Where evidence recovery cannot be bounded, retain PRV-13b’s “pager-latency figure” ([02-provider-contract.md:277](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/02-provider-contract.md#L277)); neither sum nor maximum turns repeated unavailable evidence into bounded completion. Build no second billing-stop estimator.

**Q14 — BLOCKER: choose (iii) for customer billing; reject its proposed automatic tombstone consequence.**

The precedent is explicit: a machine “still present after its date has its meter stopped and is billing the operator”; equally, “Stopping the machine’s meter is not tombstoning it” ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:545](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L545)). Your recommendation merges two decisions.

Proposed writer: persist the provider-established effective instant on the attachment when scheduling is recorded. **The existing meter**, on its first transaction at/after that instant, closes through `min(effective_date, authoritative_gone_observation)`, under tenant serialization. No operation needs to remain running. LDG-38 already requires the boundary be “re-read inside the same … serialization” ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:627](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L627)). Release remaining commitment only after every subject’s final increment closes.

Keep `released_at` for evidenced cleanup.
