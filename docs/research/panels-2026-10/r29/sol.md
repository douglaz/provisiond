**Q13 verdict — reject `max(600, 30)` as a safe reserve rule.** `PRV-36` measures dispatch to *observed effect*, not 30 seconds of lag beginning after a reply ([02-provider-contract.md:883](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/02-provider-contract.md#L883); [05-persistence.md:475](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/05-persistence.md#L475)). If the mutation reply arrives at 600 seconds and absence is recorded at 630, the end-to-end sample is 630. A genuine 630-second end-to-end bound already includes the call; adding the deadline again would double count it. A declared 30-second window does not cover that trace merely because the call deadline is 600. `OVR-19` says expiry “establishes neither the mutation’s outcome nor that billing stopped” ([00-overview.md:417](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/00-overview.md#L417)).

For Hetzner Cloud, the delete reply can carry an action that later fails; action evidence and an authoritative absence read precede the gone-write ([ADR-0037](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/docs/adr/0037-the-billing-stop-window-is-withdrawn-and-wind-down-is-sized-to-the-gone-write.md); [02-provider-contract.md:867](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/02-provider-contract.md#L867)). Robot’s `cancellation_date=now` was followed by `GET /server` returning 404 in the recorded live orders, but that observation is not a universal latency bound ([08-provider-notes.md:387](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/08-provider-notes.md#L387)). For an ambiguous non-create, `PRV-29` requires authoritative evidence for the known `external_id` ([02-provider-contract.md:281](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/02-provider-contract.md#L281)); `PRV-36` governs when a resource read is authoritative; `LDG-74` stops the customer meter at the recorded gone observation ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:524](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L524)). **Framing defect:** `OPS-27` searches a *create’s correlator* and does not resolve either delete ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:446](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L446)). The deadline is *per provider call*, so action polling and later reads may add sequential waits ([00-overview.md:417](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/00-overview.md#L417)).

Make `PRV-13b` own one enforceable dispatch-to-gone-write allowance, including the call, follow-up reads and their cadence; make `OVR-19` point to it; have `CNF-329` exercise a late reply followed by late visibility and a never-reply case. `PRV-36` must say how its observed maximum and conservative declaration support that allowance. **Third shape:** if only a separately bounded post-reply lag is available, add that *named* sequential term; then 600 + 30 applies to that model, not to the present `PRV-36` window. **Don’t build it:** where no defensible end-to-end bound exists, retain `PRV-29`’s unreduced pager-latency sizing or do not offer that prepaid product ([02-provider-contract.md:274](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/02-provider-contract.md#L274)). The repository documents day-or-part-day billing for Robot add-ons, but does **not** establish that every IP has a month-end cancellation term ([docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md:592](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md#L592)).

That read and writer need a specified automatic path: `OPS-32` currently records *machine* absence, not attachment absence ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:1329](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L1329)). **Framing defects:** setting `released_at` merely because the clock reached the date would turn a billing boundary into a claim of release; `STO-18` says a null `released_at` keeps the tombstone gate shut ([05-persistence.md:341](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/05-persistence.md#L341)). “Metered forever” overstates the gap: operator release and account termination exist, but no scheduled attachment completion path is specified ([04-api-contract.md:1067](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/04-api-contract.md#L1067); [12-billing-and-ledger.md:308](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L308)). `CNF-328` says its case “asserts no transition at the future date”; it leaves that later behavior untested, rather than defining its eventual outcome ([10-conformance-checklist.md:3124](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/0b286219c51fa5ff8ba505fefee62c0892e643ee/10-conformance-checklist.md#L3124)).
