The Manager correctly contrasts `PRV-9`’s “MUST NOT remove them until the provider has actually consumed the key material” ([02-provider-contract.md:131](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/843a2f28650922383fc355972720cd69ac743fa0/02-provider-contract.md#L131)) with `OPS-32`’s “operation is not `running`” → “deleted” ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:1422](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/843a2f28650922383fc355972720cd69ac743fa0/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L1422)). An accepted create’s failed poll reaches `needs_reconciliation` while consumption may remain pending. Your rule fixes that trace only if the sweep shares it.

The smaller rule is: **delete when registration is no longer needed—consumed, or authoritative evidence establishes no remaining consumer—and still-needed correlation survives deletion.** Apply it to ordinary, failure and sweep cleanup; preserve the sweep’s protection against racing a running worker.

- **Failure/cancellation:** delete after definitive refusal, completed cancellation or successful rescue deactivation establishes no consumer. “Cancelled” is not an operation state; see `03-operation-lifecycle.md:37`. A cancellation request alone proves nothing.
- **Unknown, abandoned, missing operation:** retain and surface the key until that evidence exists, even after settlement. `OPS-3` says `failed` asserts “nothing about its side effects”; `abandoned` reaches it “having established nothing” ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:81](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/843a2f28650922383fc355972720cd69ac743fa0/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L81)). “Delete on settlement, whatever the outcome” is therefore unsafe.
- **“Up” overstates the evidence.** V2/V18/V19 propose conservative milestones, not documented consumption guarantees (`docs/research/provider-verify-2026-10.md:133`, `:460`, `:475`). Reset acceptance is not rescue boot. V2 also misattributes unconditional immediate cleanup to `PRV-9`, whose sentence begins “For a synchronous create” (`02-provider-contract.md:134`).

Robot’s separate fingerprint-survival restriction remains at `03-operation-lifecycle.md:1428`.
