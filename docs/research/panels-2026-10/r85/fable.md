**2. Reason.** DOM-17 says "`retryable` describes whether repeating the *same request* is safe and sensible" and "It is normative guidance to callers (`API-51`)". The caller fixes the location and resubmits. The generated-name defect then surfaces as `internal` with retryable true, which is truthful for that new request. Under A the order reverses, and the caller's retries during the defect window are futile by construction while labelled sensible. On OPS-11: B keeps "a failure classified `failed` without this pre-step-9 rule keeps its stated `retryable`", so false stays, and neither form implies an automatic retry, since DOM-17 says retryable "MUST NOT be used by the system to retry automatically".
