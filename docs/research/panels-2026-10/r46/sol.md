**Verdict: Reject (b).** A changed last-rescue record is evidence of a change, not evidence that *our* reset produced it. Prefer a field-specific `Europe/Berlin` rule if the owner confirms that zone; accept an ambiguous time when **every** possible instant is later than reset.

- **Evidence is narrower than stated.** [live-session3:11](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/docs/research/panels-2026-10/r45/live-session3.txt#L11) to [live-session3:14](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/docs/research/panels-2026-10/r45/live-session3.txt#L14) is 87 seconds to port 22, not about 60; the first observed `active:false` is 44 seconds after reset, not within 30. The captures show one boot on each of two *different* servers ([live-session2:10](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/docs/research/panels-2026-10/r45/live-session2.txt#L10), [live-session3:6](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/docs/research/panels-2026-10/r45/live-session3.txt#L6)); they do not establish [ADR-0041:19](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/docs/adr/0041-robot-rescue-host-keys-are-pinned-from-the-last-rescue-record-after-boot.md#L19)’s “Every rescue boot generates fresh keys.” The recorded `boot_time` fits Berlin summer time but does not prove the field’s zone.

- **Detector failures — [PRV-19:690](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/02-provider-contract.md#L690), [CNF-355:3560](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/10-conformance-checklist.md#L3560).** Two boots can have the same second-resolution `boot_time`, including in the repeated autumn hour: (b) rejects the fresh second boot. Comparing fingerprints avoids that rejection *if* per-boot rotation is guaranteed; requiring both comparisons restores it. Conversely, another user’s boot between baseline and our reset changes either value. A delayed intermediate record can be accepted before ours appears. A reset that fails must error and invoke partial-failure handling; an accepted reset that never boots, or a retried reset after activation was consumed, can still be paired falsely with an intervening changed record. A baseline `404` proves only that this read found no record: [ADR-0041:21](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/docs/adr/0041-robot-rescue-host-keys-are-pinned-from-the-last-rescue-record-after-boot.md#L21) gives no expiry or consistency guarantee. Expiry alone does **not** imply an old record reappears; treating any later `200` as ours remains unjustified.

- **Placement — [PRV-15:659](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/02-provider-contract.md#L659), [PRV-18:679](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/02-provider-contract.md#L679).** A baseline would have to be read within begin-rescue before reset. Reading before activation widens the outside-boot race; reading after activation creates another failure needing cleanup. It need not survive a crash: [DOM-11:258](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/01-domain-model.md#L258) says a session “MUST NOT be persisted,” and [OPS-15:344](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L344) moves interrupted work to reconciliation. [PRV-20:697](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/da3e268f8d50642d56d26296e28ecccec70374dc/02-provider-contract.md#L697) forbids clearing known pins, but permits replacing them with another nonempty set; it does not repair acceptance of an intermediate record.
