**Framing corrections and M1:**

- Activation credit is not sufficient capital for any chosen machine. `PRV-13b` includes “requested_runway × customer_rate”, wind-down, fees and attachments; `PRV-13d` requires a runway floor. `LDG-9`: “available ≥ required_commitment”. For identical fresh machines, concurrency ≤ floor(balance/C); successive cycles reuse released C minus debits. Non-refundable credit remains spending authority, not profit ([02-provider-contract.md:173](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/02-provider-contract.md:173), [12-billing-and-ledger.md:215](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:215)).
- No one-minute minimum lifetime exists. `LDG-38` seeds at “that transition's recorded instant” and “posts nothing”; `LDG-74`: “Billing stops at the observation instant”, except the earlier scheduled effective date. Achievable duration depends on state transitions, latency and timestamp resolution—not cadence. `deleting` remains unspecified ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:471](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:471), [:510](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:510), [:857](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:857)).
- With p/hour and one charged provider hour: customer=.02p, loss=.98p, ratio=49; customer cost is 2.0408% of loss. Rounding, fees and differing clocks modify this. It is not 49× every deposited satoshi: another purchase still needs C. `LDG-38` prices “elapsed billable seconds” with cumulative rounding ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:623](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:623)).
- Live API prices remain unverified: unauthenticated server-types returned 401. [Hetzner’s published table](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/) disagrees: CCX63 €1.3678–1.6610/hour; your range matches dollars. CPX11’s endpoints mix old euros/new dollars; CX23 is cheaper. Corresponding illustrative loss: €1.3404–1.6278. Commercial terms are per-contract facts ([02-provider-contract.md:395](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/02-provider-contract.md:395)).
- D1 counts “20 requests per day” **per endpoint**, not successful purchases. “Every Robot sale for a day” and €1.60 are unsupported across channels/accounts, fees and margin ([02-provider-contract.md:874](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/02-provider-contract.md:874), [provider API](https://robot.hetzner.com/doc/webservice/en.html)).
- D2’s [3600/hour allowance](https://docs.hetzner.cloud/cloud.spec.json) is per Project and replenishes continuously. Worse than transient throttling: “A `stalled` episode is never retried by a timer” ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:1065](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/03-operation-lifecycle.md:1065)). Refresh/RDNS have no mandated ceiling ([Admission.lean:233](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/tools/formal/Provisiond/Admission.lean:233)).
- A minimum is not grid-independent **cost recovery**: “at least one unit” establishes a lower bound, not coverage. A wall-clock/month boundary can touch two units. Even creation-anchored rounding defeats your threshold: 61 minutes costs 2p, earns 1.22p, loses .78p. Monthly caps reduce marginal rounding but do not pool fresh machines. [DigitalOcean’s minimum](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets) is max(60 seconds’ cost,$.01); at $.00595/hour that floor spans 100.84 minutes. `PRV-13c` names “billing granularity” as per-contract ([02-provider-contract.md:395](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/02-provider-contract.md:395)).
- ADR-0011 rejected a “minimum charge of one meter interval”. Provider-unit pricing avoids cadence dependence, but retains “overcharges partial use” ([ADR-0011:88](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/docs/adr/0011-fixed-commitment-floating-runway.md:88)). The blanket Lean objection overclaims: `lifecycle_coverage` covers intervals, not money ([Funding.lean:1529](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/tools/formal/Provisiond/Funding.lean:1529)). A separate surcharge could preserve seed and release ordering; padding seconds contradicts coverage. Changing usage arithmetic changes `Funding.post` and `Witnesses.exit_closes_each_tail`’s numeric assertions ([Witnesses.lean:2049](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/tools/formal/Provisiond/Witnesses.lean:2049)).
- Respecting quarantine’s “MUST post no usage debit” and outage’s “charge the customer nothing” leaves minimum-charge exceptions; clamp remains ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:1017](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:1017), [:1308](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/12-billing-and-ledger.md:1308)). Neither corruption nor rate outage is established as tenant-triggerable; outages halt creates. Deliberate abuse can induce termination: exempting it creates a bypass; charging it changes the product.

A per-principal default multiplies through anonymous tenants; a “stated integer” already is mandatory ([07-security-requirements.md:209](/var/tmp/provisiond-panel-r19/astra/07-security-requirements.md:209)). Bound exposure across replicas/restarts; accept reduced admission under attack. **M1 verdict: A plus an aggregate exposure budget; the proposed minimum is an incomplete cure.**

Don’t build universal fair-share accounting or another tariff before measuring losses. Configured limits, restricted offers and alarms can bound accepted subsidies; terms address runtime abuse. Alarms alone cannot guarantee cleanup capacity. Admission controls preserve decided exits; minimum charges reopen pricing, reserve sizing and exception semantics.
