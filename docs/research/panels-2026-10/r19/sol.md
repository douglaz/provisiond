These ranks are **qualitative** except M1. Denied sales, account termination and operator attention have no specified monetary value; a numerical ratio or total ordering would invent one. Every row describes a risk in the specification, not an observed deployed attack. All exist independently of choosing A in this review, except that M1’s loss size depends on the customer billing rule.

**Smallest actions.** For M1 and D1, set `SEC-39`’s existing create ceiling against the most expensive admitted offer, and lower `PRV-40`’s *per-ordering-account* budget to the loss the operator accepts; put the figures on `OVR-19`. Alarm before that budget runs out. A per-tenant share alone is weak: anonymous customers can fund multiple tenants. For D2, extend the provider-budget rule in `PRV-40` to **all provider calls**, with an account/project reserve for deletes, reconciliation and sweeps; give `refresh` an explicit `SEC-39` ceiling and strengthen `API-29` where needed. This preserves `OPS-32`’s rule that an interrupted sweep “MUST record nothing about absence” ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:1289](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L1289)). For D3, admit against measured provider quota by account, including temporary images; use `RSC-42`’s required purge and sweep before adding a new attachment mechanism ([06-rescue-install.md:472](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/06-rescue-install.md#L472)). For D4, use `SEC-41`–`SEC-45`’s terms, account distribution, notice handling and suspension; verify account separation as `SEC-44` requires ([07-security-requirements.md:323](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/07-security-requirements.md#L323)). None of these changes needs to weaken a Lean billing theorem. The strongest reason **not** to build further machinery is that account suspension and Sybil resistance cannot be guaranteed by counters; provider terms, stated deployment budgets and an operator alarm carry those decisions more honestly.

**M1 verdict:** **A plus an account-level loss budget and the existing per-principal ceiling**, not a one-provider-unit minimum. At 60 seconds and 20% margin, customer charge is `0.02p`, operator loss `0.98p`: **49 times the charge**. There is no specified minimum positive billable life: `LDG-38` seeds at the recorded billable transition and closes at its stop; `LDG-74` generally stops at the gone-observation instant ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:859](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L859), [12-billing-and-ledger.md:510](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L510)). Satoshi ceiling rounding may impose a one-satoshi debit, but it is no provider-cost floor. A create ties up the **full** `PRV-13b` reserve while open, bounding concurrency through `available ≥ required_commitment`; release lets the same remaining balance fund another cycle ([02-provider-contract.md:172](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/02-provider-contract.md#L172), [12-billing-and-ledger.md:215](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L215)).

**Defects in the framing.**

- The minimum is not grid independent as a *recovery rule*. A short life crossing a wall-clock boundary can incur two rounded units; a monthly cap can reduce incremental cost; DigitalOcean’s charge is the greater of 60 seconds or **$0.01**, and its 672-hour cap applies to bundled plans, not every configuration ([Hetzner billing](https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/billing/faq/), [DigitalOcean pricing](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/droplets/details/pricing/)). The claim that margin covers every life longer than `U/(1+m)` is false just after a second unit starts.
- `ADR-0011` rejected a **meter-interval** minimum specifically because it “overcharges partial use” and makes cadence a price input ([ADR-0011:85](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/docs/adr/0011-fixed-commitment-floating-runway.md#L85)). A provider-unit minimum avoids the cadence objection but retains the partial-use objection. As a usage debit it changes `LDG-38`’s formula and `Witnesses.exit_closes_each_tail`’s expected entries ([Witnesses.lean:2044](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/tools/formal/Provisiond/Witnesses.lean#L2044)). `Funding.lifecycle_coverage` proves **interval coverage, including zero-entry increments**, not a positive charge ([Funding.lean:1529](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/tools/formal/Provisiond/Funding.lean#L1529)). A separate fee would need its own advance authorization and reserve. Quarantine explicitly posts “no usage debit” ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:1014](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L1014)); outage absorption and the commitment clamp likewise defeat an unconditional exit charge ([12-billing-and-ledger.md:270](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L270)).
- The price range is not verified by unauthenticated `GET /v1/server_types`: that call requires a project token. Hetzner’s published table labels some quoted endpoints **old prices or dollars**; its new euro CCX63 range reaches €1.6610, making roughly €1.63 loss for that one-minute example ([Hetzner price table](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/), [API authentication](https://docs.hetzner.cloud/reference/cloud)). The asserted €1.60 to exhaust Robot orders has no common price basis: offers vary and standard orders can carry setup fees ([08-provider-notes.md:135](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/08-provider-notes.md#L135)). `OVR-19` calls the order budget “per ordering account,” despite `PRV-40`’s “whole deployment” wording ([00-overview.md:380](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/00-overview.md#L380), [02-provider-contract.md:879](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/02-provider-contract.md#L879)).
- Hetzner’s documented 3,600/hour Cloud limit **replenishes gradually**, so a burst is not a day-long lockout; `403 resource_limit_exceeded` is documented, but actual quota and reachability require measurement ([Hetzner API](https://docs.hetzner.cloud/reference/cloud)). D4’s rapid-churn trigger remains conjecture; `SEC-41`’s proven concern is broader workload abuse, not a documented IP-cycling sanction ([07-security-requirements.md:174](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/40c00cb0f16177b4a49fad3e2357f74dae11902d/07-security-requirements.md#L174)).

Both required gates passed; the tracked tree is unchanged.
