**Q17a — Real specification ambiguity; accept structural startup validation.**

The register is **not uniformly scalar-only**. Request-budget validation compares against “the driver’s declaration” ([00-overview.md:377](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/00-overview.md#L377)); `STO-55` explicitly “reads `max_connections` at startup”—live database configuration ([05-persistence.md:180](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/05-persistence.md#L180)). **Q17b — Real dispatch-boundary question; accept local failure, with an explicit cutoff.**

When B’s closure is visible before A’s first provider call, fail A’s **existing operation** with `rate_limited` and `retry_after_ms` equal to the remaining local closure interval. This is a worker result. `OPS-11` currently calls local closure “an admission occurrence”; its admission-only kinds “MUST NOT be emitted by a worker or a driver” ([03-operation-lifecycle.md:234](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L234), [:254](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L254)). Amend the occurrence’s scope; do not add `rate_limited` to that kind list or fabricate upstream `status:403`.

**The precedent has limits:** `API-63` checks at “the last write before the ordering call,” potentially after ancillary provider calls, and expressly allows termination “between the write and the call” ([04-api-contract.md:1398](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/04-api-contract.md#L1398)). Sending A can waste listing/key/order traffic. **Another 403 is not certain:** upstream “window reset” remains “unverified” ([02-provider-contract.md:1030](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/02-provider-contract.md#L1030)). Deferring initial dispatch until reopening is a third shape—not a repeated mutation—but retains commitments and delays potentially stale purchases. **Don’t build a channel scheduler or network-spanning lock for this.**

**Q17c — Real wire-contract gap; ordinary `409 conflict`/`state`, but recommend `retryable: true`.**

Two tenants race for the same account/channel/offer. The loser gets “without a commitment, operation or receipt” ([02-provider-contract.md:1095](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/02-provider-contract.md#L1095)). It has purchased nothing. Repeating its unchanged request/key can succeed if the winner fails; if the winner succeeds, the offer’s absence prevents purchase. Treating the winner’s uncertainty as the loser’s duplicate-purchase risk is the framing defect.

`DOM-17` asks whether repeating “the same request” is “safe and sensible” ([01-domain-model.md:642](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/01-domain-model.md#L642)). Temporary contention meets that test; success need not be guaranteed. Re-listing before resending is a reasonable caller strategy, not grounds for permanently declaring the request nonretryable.

Return the ordinary error, with no manufactured `refusals`/`not_checked` list. `API-7` collects from “one read-only snapshot” and separately retains conditional-write guards ([04-api-contract.md:261](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/04-api-contract.md#L261)). No invented release-time estimate is needed. `API-50`’s promise concerns “the deployment’s caller-throughput pacing,” not success against resource contention ([04-api-contract.md:1207](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/04-api-contract.md#L1207)).
