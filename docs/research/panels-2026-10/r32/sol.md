**Q17b — Real question; stop A locally if closure is recorded before its first provider call.** A already has an operation, so this cannot be the [API-7 tail](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/04-api-contract.md#L283), whose refusal commits no operation. This is the brief’s principal framing defect. A should settle `failed` with `rate_limited` and the closure’s remaining `retry_after_ms`; its commitment is released, and the setup fee and prepaid first unit are never debited for a deterministic rejection before acceptance ([LDG-32](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L308), [LDG-39](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/12-billing-and-ledger.md#L1220)). Specify the operation view’s `retryable: true`: no purchase was attempted, though retrying after expiry is a **new** operation, not a replay of A.

[API-63](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/04-api-contract.md#L1397) requires the worker to “repeat the admission check as a conditional write” for account health; it does not check closure. Add a shared closure check at dispatch, including calls preceding the order, and a conformance race with B’s recorded 403. Sending A spends provider requests and may get an order-request 403; a throttle *after acceptance* instead invokes [OPS-11’s acceptance rule](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/03-operation-lifecycle.md#L268) and can hold funds for reconciliation. “Another 403” is not certain: [PRV-40](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/02-provider-contract.md#L1030) says Robot’s scope and reset are unverified, so stopping A can also lose an order opportunity. That is the policy cost. Do not park A until an inferred reset or promise that a check eliminates the race between check and call. The existing [Lean tail model](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/tools/formal/Provisiond/Admission.lean#L1026) covers snapshots, not worker dispatch; no theorem needs weakening.

**Q17c — Real wire question; ordinary `409 conflict`/`state`, but I recommend `retryable: true`.** The guard loser has “no commitment, operation or receipt” ([PRV-42](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/02-provider-contract.md#L1095)). If the winner fails, that same offer may remain purchasable; if it succeeds, the listing check prevents a second purchase. A later attempt is therefore safe and can be sensible under [DOM-17’s](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/01-domain-model.md#L642) “same request” test. `true` gives no immediate-retry instruction or invented `retry_after_ms`; no release time is known. The brief’s false-retryability rationale treats the winner’s possible success as though the loser had already bought something. Keep this refusal outside the rich tail: [API-7](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/04-api-contract.md#L261) collects a read-only snapshot, which cannot win the conditional write. [API-50’s](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/04-api-contract.md#L1207) promise concerns caller-throughput `429`s, not this `409`. Add the non-tail retryability rule and exact ordinary response to [WIR-9a](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/13-wire-contract.md#L157) and [CNF-343](https://github.com/douglaz/provisiond/blob/7d21478c29e2a82d6540bd7a84b5a8dc5351dc7b/10-conformance-checklist.md#L3373); no Lean tail variant is needed.
