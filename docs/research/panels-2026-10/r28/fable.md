**Verdict: amend.** Keep the effective order and the skip, but reclassify the channel refusal as a pool entry, fold the offer refusal into the sellability refusal the set already owes a kind to, and fix two edits the brief lists wrongly or omits.

**Why.** ADR-0030 line 10 already supplies the ordering rule: "the resource before the pool, then pricing and spending". Nothing new is needed once each refusal is classified. A create names two resources, the account and the offer, and the offer is listed under the account, so account precedes offer. The ordering channel is not something the request names. ADR-0036 line 37 closes it for an account and channel, shared by every tenant there, which is the same shape as the solvency halt: a pool. It goes first in the pool group because it is the narrower pool, which keeps the headline on the fault closest to the request. The offer refusal is not new. PRV-31 at 02-provider-contract.md:431 says "an offer with **no declared bound MUST NOT be sold on prepaid terms**", CNF-166 at 10-conformance-checklist.md:2958 requires that create "refused before any provider call", and neither names a kind or a position. ADR-0039 line 32 calls itself "`PRV-31`'s rule carried over". So the edit gives one existing refusal a kind and a tail slot, and ADR-0039 adds a second predicate to "sellable". Kind: `conflict` with `details.reason: "offer_not_sellable"`, retryable false. WIR-29 at 13-wire-contract.md:944-946 settles the `not_found` question: a resource omitted from the listing is still refused at create by its own reason, because "omission from the listing is not by itself the control". The price-check skip is right, and its justification already exists at 04-api-contract.md:269-271: cap and balance must not run against an unhealthy account because WIR-30 404s that listing. ADR-0039 line 72 filters unsellable offers from the same listing, so the same rule applies, and the fenced extension shows the set skips only when the price is not legitimately served, not merely because a resource refused. The ordering is a real wire question: it fixes HTTP status, `kind`, `Retry-After` and the envelope's pacing. But the permanent-versus-transient framing is one ADR-0030 lines 9-11 already rejected, and WIR-9b's conjunction carries the permanence signal whatever the headline.

**Cases** (rate present unless stated; cap supplied):

| Case | `refusals` in order | Headline | `not_checked` reasons |
|---|---|---|---|
| unhealthy account + unsellable offer | `conflict`/`state`, `conflict`/`offer_not_sellable` | 409, envelope false | `provider_account_not_healthy`, `offer_not_sellable` |

| healthy, closed channel + solvency halt | `rate_limited`, `halted`/`solvency` | 429 with channel pacing; solvency pacing only in its entry; envelope true | none |

Skipping the price checks hides nothing an agent can use: cap and balance are computed from this offer's price, which the agent cannot buy at and the listing does not show it. Halt and rate stay reported because they outlive the offer choice.

**(B) is a tail refusal.** Step 5c sits before the tail because it spends a ceiling slot and must follow the replay check, per 04-api-contract.md:245-250. The channel gate reads a stored "closed until" fact, spends nothing and writes nothing, which is the tail's purity condition at line 262-263. API-41 is the unauthenticated enrol route and is not comparable. Placing it at 5c would stop at the first refusal and hide the halt, the outcome ADR-0030 exists to prevent.

**Defects in the framing.**
- brief.md:35 puts the channel in "the request's own resource refusal". ADR-0030 line 10 and ADR-0036 line 37 make it a pool. Wire effect nil; wording effect real, since API-7's clause and ADR-0030's rationale are what the next refusal is placed by.
- brief.md:15 and ADR-0036 line 37 say the kind "joins `OPS-11`'s admission-only list". 03-operation-lifecycle.md:237-239 defines that list as kinds that "MUST NOT be emitted by a worker or a driver", and 02-provider-contract.md:50 requires drivers to emit `rate_limited`. The kind cannot join. The occurrence classifies `failed` under the create row's "otherwise `failed`" with `accepted: false`, one sentence, no list change.
- The brief treats (A) as a new refusal. 02-provider-contract.md:431 and 10-conformance-checklist.md:2958 already require it without a kind.
- API-50 is missing from the edits. 04-api-contract.md:1195-1200 enumerates the 429s that "arrive on a caller's very first request"; the channel refusal is a new member and needs the sentence, or CNF-152's promise reads as broken by another tenant's orders.
- Item 1's headline question is answered by WIR-9b at 13-wire-contract.md:200-202, not by a preference between the two.

**Smaller shape and the strongest "don't".** Do not add a second sellability rule, a details key naming the account or channel, a channel echo, or a `ceiling_exceeded` sibling. One `sellable` predicate on the offer owned by PRV-44, listed by WIR-30, refused by API-7. The one alternative worth naming is running cap and balance against an unsellable offer, which saves a reason value and a Lean constructor at the cost of reporting a price WIR-30 withholds; reject it for consistency with 04:269-271.
